ACORN Utilities

Tools for preparing files before a deployment. They run entirely in your browser: nothing is uploaded, and no account or install is needed.

Audio converter

Convert WAV, MP3 and other audio to the WAV format ACORN plays (16-bit PCM, mono, 44.1 or 48 kHz). Choose the channel, trim the clip and preview it before you download.

Log Plotter

Open a playback or environment log from the SD card to see its key numbers and plots. Copy a text summary to share, or print the page to PDF.
